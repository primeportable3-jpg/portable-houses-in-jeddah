# What Are Portable Houses?

Portable houses are prefabricated structures designed to provide functional living or working spaces while offering flexibility in placement and use. These buildings can be designed in different sizes and layouts depending on the intended purpose.

Unlike conventional construction, portable buildings can offer a more adaptable approach to creating additional space. They can be suitable for residential projects, temporary facilities, offices, accommodation, and other applications where practical space is required.

Before selecting a portable building, it is important to understand the specific purpose of the space. Consider the required size, number of occupants, interior layout, materials, insulation, ventilation, electrical requirements, doors, windows, and other features.
